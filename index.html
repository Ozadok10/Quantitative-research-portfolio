SELECTED WORK

Research projects

Two empirical studies combining financial-market data, statistical testing, and interactive dashboards.

01 / INTRADAY MARKETS
SPY · QQQ   /   INTRADAY ANALYSIS

Can Historical Market Patterns Predict Intraday Direction?

Tested whether technical indicators, momentum and mean-reversion patterns, Markov models, and time-to-event analysis improve forecasts of short-term market direction.

KEY FINDING

Under the tested conditions, common historical patterns and indicators offered limited reliable predictive improvement.

02 / MACRO & RISK
2Y · 10Y · 30Y TREASURIES   /   SPY · IWM

Treasury Yield Curve & Equity Volatility

Studied how five-session Treasury yield changes relate to subsequent five-session realized volatility in SPY and IWM from 2010 through 2025.

KEY FINDING

Sharp yield declines were associated with higher subsequent realized volatility than sharp increases in several tested conditions. Associations are not causal conclusions.
